# Cast

Target shape, after Duolingo: **one main character and six side characters**. The product is known by the main character; the side characters have stable identities of their own and never compete with it.

The drawings come from Sparkles’ `/design/nix` bench. The 52 practice-item characters, the human candidates and Mabel are not part of the cast. The koala is not part of the cast.

## Main character

| Character | Signature |
|---|---|
| **Firefly** | A tail that glows |

The firefly is decided. It is the face of the product: it must stay recognisable on the first screen and the tenth, so its silhouette, palette and signature do not change between contexts. Only pose, expression and authored wardrobe do. Its glow is a signature, not a status: it must never read as “correct” or “approved”.

A side character must be told apart from the main character and from every other side character by silhouette alone, at small size, in one flat colour. Give each a distinct signature and a palette that does not sit in the firefly’s glow-yellow or in a status hue.

Which side character plays which role in the product (practice, guide, seasonal moments) is not decided. Record it here when it is. Open slots are filled by the user’s choice of animal; do not invent them unprompted.
